# Legal Compliance Checklist (12 Dimensions)

Use this checklist at two points for any project:
1. **Before starting** — after design is approved, before writing code
2. **Before launching** — before deploying, publishing, or delivering

For each dimension, mark "Clear" or "Risk" with a risk level.

| # | Dimension | Check |
|---|-----------|-------|
| 1 | **Content Compliance** | No illegal content (violence, gambling, fraud, terrorism, etc.) |
| 2 | **Intellectual Property** | All code, fonts, images, music, data have valid licenses (open-source/commercial/self-created) |
| 3 | **Privacy Protection** | Is user data collected? Is it necessary? Is there a consent mechanism? Is storage encrypted? Is there a deletion path? |
| 4 | **Data Security** | Where is data stored? Is transmission encrypted (HTTPS)? Any risk of leaks or unauthorized access? |
| 5 | **Qualification Requirements** | Does this project type need special licenses? (ICP, broadcasting, payment, medical, education, etc.) |
| 6 | **Industry Regulation** | Is this a heavily regulated industry? (finance, healthcare, education, news, gaming, social media, etc.) |
| 7 | **User Agreements** | Are terms of service, privacy policy, and disclaimers needed? Are they complete and compliant? |
| 8 | **Age Restrictions** | Is the content suitable for minors? Is age verification needed? |
| 9 | **Advertising Compliance** | Does it involve ads or marketing? Does it comply with advertising laws? |
| 10 | **Cross-Border Compliance** | Does it involve cross-border data transfer or multi-region users? (GDPR, COPPA, etc.) |
| 11 | **Third-Party Dependencies** | Are all APIs/SDKs/libraries compliant for this use case? Any supply chain risks? |
| 12 | **Potential Controversy** | Any content that could be controversial? (political, religious, racial, gender, misinformation) |

## Risk Levels

- **Red**: Must consult a professional lawyer before proceeding. Do NOT continue.
- **Yellow**: Must get explicit user confirmation before continuing.
- **Green**: Proceed.

## Rule

- **No assessment = no start.** Complete before writing any code.
- **No assessment = no launch.** Complete before deploying or publishing.
- **Don't fake legal knowledge.** If unsure, say "this is beyond my knowledge, consult a lawyer."
